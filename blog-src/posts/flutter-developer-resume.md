Most companies in India now use an **applicant tracking system (ATS)** to collect and filter resumes. Before a recruiter reads yours, software reads it, extracts your details and ranks you against the job description. A beautiful two-column resume made in a design tool can come out as jumbled text, and you never hear back.

This guide shows how to write a Flutter developer resume that software can read and humans want to read.

## The ATS-friendly format

Follow these rules and almost every ATS will read your resume correctly:

- **One column.** No side panels, text boxes or tables for layout.
- **Real text.** Never put text inside images, icons or charts. Skill bars and star ratings mean nothing to an ATS.
- **Standard section names**: Professional Summary, Technical Skills, Professional Experience, Projects, Education, Certifications.
- **Consistent dates**, like "Jun 2023 – Present".
- **A PDF made from text**, not a scan or a photo.
- **One page** for freshers and up to about 4 years of experience. Two pages are fine for senior developers.

## The right section order

**For experienced developers:**

1. Name, title and contact line (city, phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub)
2. Professional Summary (2–3 lines)
3. Technical Skills (grouped)
4. Professional Experience
5. Projects
6. Education
7. Certifications

**For freshers:** move **Projects** above Experience. Your apps are your experience.

## Write a summary that says something

A weak summary:

> Passionate and hardworking Flutter developer looking for opportunities to grow.

A strong summary:

> Flutter developer with 2 years of experience building consumer apps with Bloc, Firebase and REST APIs. Shipped 3 apps to the Play Store with 20,000+ combined downloads. Focused on clean architecture, testing and smooth performance.

The difference is **specifics**: years, tools, results.

## Group your skills the way recruiters scan

Don't paste one long list. Group skills so both the ATS and the recruiter find them fast:

- **Languages & Frameworks:** Flutter, Dart, Kotlin
- **State Management:** Bloc, Provider, Riverpod
- **Backend & Integrations:** Firebase (Auth, Firestore, FCM), REST APIs, Google Maps
- **Architecture & Practices:** Clean Architecture, MVVM, unit and widget testing, CI/CD, Git

Use the **same words as the job description**. If the job says "Riverpod" and you know it, write "Riverpod", not just "state management".

## Bullet points that get interviews

Every bullet should answer: *what did you build, with what, and what changed?* Start with a strong verb and add a number when you can.

**Weak:**

- Worked on Flutter app
- Responsible for bug fixing
- Used Firebase

**Strong:**

- Built real-time order tracking with Google Maps and Firebase for a food delivery app with 50,000+ downloads
- Cut the crash rate from 2.1% to 0.3% by adding error handling, logging and widget tests
- Migrated the app to Bloc and clean architecture, reducing time to ship new features by about 30%
- Integrated Razorpay payments and push notifications with Firebase Cloud Messaging

No big numbers yet? Use what you have: screens built, API endpoints integrated, test coverage, app size reduced, rating improved, users at your college who use your app.

## The projects section (most important for freshers)

For each project, give one line on what it does and the tech, plus a link:

> **QuickBite**, food delivery app · Flutter, Bloc, Firebase, Google Maps · play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=...
> - Live rider tracking, cart, payments and order history; 500+ downloads

A Play Store link or a GitHub repository with a good README is the strongest proof on any fresher resume.

## Common mistakes

1. **A photo, date of birth, father's name and marital status.** Not needed for software roles in India, and they take space. Leave the photo off unless the job asks for one.
2. **"Objective" instead of a summary.** Recruiters want to know what you offer, not what you want.
3. **Listing every technology you have heard of.** Only list what you can discuss in an interview.
4. **Skill bars and ratings** ("Flutter ★★★★☆"). ATS can't read them and recruiters don't trust them.
5. **No links.** Always include GitHub, LinkedIn and app links.
6. **One resume for every job.** Adjust your summary and skills to each job description. It takes five minutes and makes a big difference.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best resume format for a Flutter developer?

A single-column, reverse-chronological resume with standard section names, grouped skills, and projects with links. For freshers, put projects before experience.

### Should I add a photo to my resume?

For software jobs in India, usually no. Most companies and ATS systems don't need it. Add one only if the job posting asks for it.

### How long should a Flutter developer resume be?

One page for freshers and developers with up to about four years of experience. Senior developers can use two pages.
