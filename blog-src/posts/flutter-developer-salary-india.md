"How much does a Flutter developer earn?" is one of the most searched questions about Flutter in India, and most answers online quote a single number with no source. This guide uses published salary data, explains why numbers vary so much, and shows what you can do to earn at the top of the range.

## The short answer

Our estimate, based on the published salary data below and the offers we see in current job postings:

| Experience | Typical yearly salary (CTC) | Roughly per month |
|---|---|---|
| Fresher / intern converting to full-time | ₹2.5 – 4.5 lakh | ₹20,000 – 37,000 |
| 1 – 4 years | ₹4 – 8 lakh | ₹33,000 – 67,000 |
| 5 – 9 years | ₹8 – 15 lakh | ₹67,000 – 1.25 lakh |
| Lead / senior at product companies | ₹15 – 30 lakh+ | ₹1.25 lakh+ |

These are **typical ranges**, not limits. Product companies in Bengaluru, fintechs and well-funded startups pay well above them. Small agencies in smaller cities often pay below.

## What the salary sites say

Salary sites collect self-reported numbers, so each one gives a slightly different picture. Here is what they show for Flutter developers in India:

- **PayScale** (124 salary profiles, updated June 2026): average base salary of about **₹4.9 lakh** a year. Early-career developers (1–4 years) average about **₹4.9 lakh** total pay, and mid-career developers (5–9 years) about **₹8 lakh**. The middle 80% earn between roughly ₹1.25 lakh and ₹9 lakh base.
- **Glassdoor** (50 salaries reported): average of about **₹4.7 lakh** a year, with most reports between ₹3 lakh and ₹7.6 lakh.
- **Glassdoor, freshers only** (a small sample of 9 reports): around **₹40,000 a month** on average, with a wide spread.

Two cautions. First, these samples are small, so a few high or low reports move the average a lot. Second, people at top product companies rarely report on these sites, so the published averages tend to undercount the top end. Use them as a floor for negotiation, not a ceiling.

## Why Flutter salaries vary so much

### 1. The type of company

This is the biggest factor, bigger than your exact years of experience.

- **Service companies and agencies** build apps for clients. They hire many freshers and pay at the lower end, but give you broad exposure quickly.
- **Startups** pay mid-range in cash, sometimes with stock options.
- **Product companies and fintechs** (where the app is the business) pay the most and expect clean architecture, testing and performance skills.

### 2. The city

Bengaluru, Gurugram, Hyderabad and Mumbai pay the most. Remote roles for Indian companies usually pay close to the company's home-city rate. Remote roles for foreign companies can pay much more, but expect strong communication skills and 3+ years of experience.

### 3. Proof that you can ship

Two developers with the same three years can be ₹5 lakh apart. The one with published apps, a clean GitHub and measurable results ("cut crash rate from 2% to 0.3%", "app has 50,000 downloads") gets the higher offer.

### 4. Skills beyond basic Flutter

Skills that consistently push salaries up:

- **State management at scale** (Bloc or Riverpod, with a clear architecture)
- **Testing**: unit, widget and integration tests
- **Native knowledge**: Kotlin or Swift for platform channels and SDKs
- **CI/CD**: automated builds and releases with Codemagic, GitHub Actions or Fastlane
- **Performance work**: profiling with DevTools, reducing jank and app size

## How Flutter salaries compare with React Native and native

PayScale's 2026 data for early-career mobile developers with React Native skills shows an average of about **₹5.7 lakh**, slightly above the Flutter average. The samples are small and the difference is within the normal spread, so the honest conclusion is: **the framework matters less than the company and your proof of work.** Developers who know Flutter *and* some native Android or iOS are paid well in either world. ## How to move into a higher salary band

1. **Ship one app you are proud of** and put the link at the top of your resume.
2. **Write down your impact with numbers** in every role. Recruiters and ATS software both reward specifics. Our [resume guide](flutter-developer-resume.html) shows how.
3. **Learn testing and one architecture deeply.** These are the most common gaps we see in interviews.
4. **Switch company type, not just company.** Moving from an agency to a product company is often the biggest single jump.
5. **Negotiate with data.** Quote the ranges above and your competing offers, politely and in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the salary of a Flutter developer fresher in India?

Most freshers start between ₹2.5 lakh and ₹4.5 lakh a year (about ₹20,000 to ₹37,000 a month). Freshers with a published app and strong fundamentals can get offers at the upper end or above, especially at startups.

### Is Flutter a good career in 2026?

Yes. Flutter is the most-used cross-platform framework in JetBrains' developer surveys for four years running, and Google reports it is used for nearly 30% of new free iOS apps. Demand in India is steady across agencies, startups and product companies.

### How can I increase my salary as a Flutter developer?

Ship real apps, learn testing and clean architecture, add basic Kotlin or Swift, and show measurable results on your resume. Moving from a service company to a product company usually brings the biggest jump.
