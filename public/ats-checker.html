Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The ATS check is free with no sign-up and no limit. Resume Premium, which is optional, adds AI tailoring and cover letters for people who build their resume on Widgetry.

Do you store my resume?

No. Your file is opened and scored inside your browser, on your device. It isn't sent to our servers.

Why is my score different on another site?

Every ATS and every checker weighs things a little differently. This check follows what most ATS look for: readable text, standard headings, the job's keywords and clear achievements. Fix the tips it shows and your resume will read better everywhere.

My PDF scored 0 on "ATS can read it". Why?

The PDF is probably a scanned image or exported from a design tool as pictures. ATS can't read pictures. Export a text PDF from Word or Google Docs, or build an ATS-friendly resume with the free Widgetry resume builder.
