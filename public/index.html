Questions

Frequently asked

Are classes online or offline?

Live online. You join from home on your laptop, code along with me in real time and ask questions as we go. There are no pre-recorded videos.

How long is each session?

2 hours each, on Saturdays and Sundays: 10 sessions over 5 weekends, 20 hours in total. Exact timings for each batch are on the batch card above.

I've never written code. Can I join?

Yes. Days 1 and 2 cover setup and Dart from the beginning. If you can use a computer comfortably, you can follow along.

What do I need?

A laptop (Windows, macOS or Linux, 8 GB RAM recommended), a stable internet connection, and an Android phone if you have one. Any stream, from Class 10 to graduation, can join.

Do I need a Mac or an iPhone?

No. Any Windows, macOS or Linux laptop works. We build and test on Android; the same code runs on iOS.

What if I miss a session?

Message me and I'll help you catch up before the next class.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, after you complete and present your project on Day 10.

How do I pay? Is it safe?

Payments are handled by Razorpay, India's widely used payment gateway. You can pay with UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking, and you get a receipt by email.

What if I can't continue?

You get a full refund if you cancel before Session 2, and 50% before Session 3. See the refund policy.

Why Flutter?

One codebase gives you Android, iOS and web apps. Big products like Tata Neu use it, and it's one of the fastest ways for a beginner to get a real app onto a phone.
