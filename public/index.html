The syllabus

Five weekends, one finished app

Built for beginners. One app, built step by step, with something new running on your phone after every session.

Download full syllabus & brochure (PDF)

Weekend1

Day 1 · Saturday

Setup & your first app

Install Flutter and VS Code, create a project, and run it on your own phone.

Day 2 · Sunday

Dart basics for Flutter

Variables, functions, lists and classes: just what you need to build apps.

Weekend2

Day 3 · Saturday

Widgets & layouts

Row, Column, Container and images. Build your app's home screen.

Day 4 · Sunday

Buttons, input & state

Make the screen respond to taps and typing with setState.

Weekend3

Day 5 · Saturday

Lists & navigation

Scrolling lists and moving between screens with a detail page.

Day 6 · Sunday

Getting data from the internet

Call a real API and show live data in your app.

Weekend4

Day 7 · Saturday

Forms & saving data

Build a form and save favourites so they stay after restart.

Day 8 · Sunday

Firebase login & database

Add sign-in and save user data to the cloud.

Weekend5

Day 9 · Saturday

Polish your app

Theme, app icon, splash screen and fixing bugs together.

Day 10 · Sunday

Build, share & demo day

Build your APK, upload code to GitHub and present your app.

Yes. Days 1 and 2 cover setup and Dart from the beginning. If you can use a computer comfortably, you can follow along.

No. Any Windows, macOS or Linux laptop works. We build and test on Android; the same code runs on iOS.

Message me and I'll help you catch up before the next class.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, after you complete and present your project on Day 10.
