Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Widgetry Academy, operated by Kishan Kumar Sharma, respects your privacy. This policy covers our website and the Widgetry Academy mobile app, and explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Registration details: name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, class or course, coding experience and the course you're interested in.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us. We use only your name and email to create your registration. We never see your Google password, and you can remove our access any time from your Google Account settings.
  • Payment details: handled by Razorpay or, in the Android app, by Google Play if you choose it. We receive the payment status, amount, payment ID and the details you enter at checkout (name, email, phone). We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login. When you pay through Razorpay in the Android app, we report the sale (amount, date and your state, for tax) to Google, as Google Play requires.
  • Basic technical data: our website runs on Google Firebase, which may process standard data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site.
  • Website analytics: we use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used, such as how many people register. These reports don't identify you personally. You can block these cookies in your browser settings.

Widgetry Jobs and the Talent Network

  • Talent Network profiles: if you create one, we store your name, email, phone (optional), headline, experience, skills, city, links (GitHub, LinkedIn, portfolio, apps), job preferences and, if you add it, expected salary.
  • Who sees it: your profile is private. Our team reviews it to verify your skills. We share it with an employer only after you approve, and never with companies you list under "never show my profile to".
  • Job posts: when an employer posts a job, we store the job details and the poster's contact name and email. Contact details are used only for our review and are not shown publicly.
  • Reports: when you report a job we store the reason and a one-way, salted hash of your IP address to prevent duplicate reports. We don't store the IP address itself.
  • Delete any time: use "Delete my profile" on the Talent Network page to erase your profile immediately, or contact us.

The mobile app

  • Account: the app uses the same Google sign-in, registration, Talent Network and payment details described above.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store a notification token for your phone (through Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send job alerts and updates about your profile, posts and payments. Turn them off any time in your phone's settings.
  • On your phone only: saved jobs, your state for payments, checklist ticks and app settings are stored on your phone and never sent to us.
  • Photos and files: the app opens your photos only when you choose a profile photo, and uploads only the photo you pick.
  • Assistant: the in-app assistant answers from built-in answers on your phone. What you type is not sent to us or to any AI service.
  • No advertising: the app has no ads and no advertising trackers, and doesn't collect your location, contacts or device identifiers for tracking.

How we use it

  • To confirm your seat and send batch dates, timings and session links.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp, phone or email about your course.
  • To issue your certificate.
  • To process payments and refunds.

What we don't do

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with services needed to run the course (Google Firebase and Google Cloud for sign-in, data storage and notifications, Google Analytics for website visit statistics, Razorpay and Google Play for payments) or when required by law.

Students under 18

If you are under 18, please register with your parent's or guardian's permission.

How long we keep it

We keep your registration details while your course is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for records and certificates. Payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires (currently about 6 years). Everything else is deleted when you delete your account.

Your choices

Delete your account: in the app go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, or use our account deletion page. It takes effect immediately.

To access or correct your information, or to stop receiving messages, contact us using the details on our Contact page.
