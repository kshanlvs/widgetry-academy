Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Widgetry Academy, operated by Kishan Kumar Sharma, respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Registration details: name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, class or course, coding experience and the course you're interested in.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us. We use only your name and email to create your registration. We never see your Google password, and you can remove our access any time from your Google Account settings.
  • Payment details: handled by Razorpay. We receive the payment status, amount and the details you enter at checkout (name, email, phone). We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • Basic technical data: our website runs on Google Firebase, which may process standard data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site.
  • Website analytics: we use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used, such as how many people register. These reports don't identify you personally. You can block these cookies in your browser settings.

How we use it

  • To confirm your seat and send batch dates, timings and session links.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp, phone or email about your course.
  • To issue your certificate.
  • To process payments and refunds.

What we don't do

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with services needed to run the course (Google Firebase for sign-in and data storage, Google Analytics for visit statistics, Razorpay for payments) or when required by law.

Students under 18

If you are under 18, please register with your parent's or guardian's permission.

How long we keep it

We keep your registration details while your course is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for records and certificates. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your choices

To access, correct or delete your information, or to stop receiving messages, contact us using the details on our Contact page.
