Posting rules & terms

Last updated: 15 October 2026. Widgetry Jobs is run by Widgetry Academy (Kishan Kumar Sharma), Kolkata, India · Udyam UDYAM-WB-22-0009252.

For job seekers

  • Widgetry Jobs is free for job seekers. We never charge you to see, apply for or get a job.
  • No genuine employer asks candidates to pay a fee, deposit or "training charge". If anyone does, don't pay, and report the job.
  • You apply directly with the company. Widgetry is not the employer and doesn't guarantee a job, interview or salary.
  • Your Talent Network profile is private. We share it with an employer only with your approval, and you can delete it any time.

Rules for posting a job

  • Jobs must be real, currently open, and involve Flutter, Dart or mobile app development, in India or remote for India-based candidates.
  • Never ask candidates for money: no registration, training, security or joining fees. Posts that do are removed and the account is blocked.
  • Give accurate details: company name, role, location, experience and, where possible, salary.
  • No discriminatory requirements (for example religion, caste or gender, unless the law allows it for a specific role).
  • Every post is reviewed before going live. We may edit, reject or remove any post, and listings expire after 30 days unless renewed.
  • Use candidates' details only for hiring for the role you posted.

Terms of use

  • Information on Widgetry Jobs is provided by employers. We review posts but can't verify every detail. Please do your own checks before accepting an offer.
  • Don't post anything unlawful, misleading, harmful or that infringes anyone's rights, and don't misuse the site (spam, scraping, fake accounts).
  • We may suspend accounts that break these rules.
  • Personal data is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
