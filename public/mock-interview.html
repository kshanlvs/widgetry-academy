Questions

Who takes the interview?

A working Flutter engineer who interviews developers. You see their name, role and experience before you book.

Which level should I pick?

Fresher if you have up to a year of experience (or none), mid level for 2–4 years, senior for 5 or more. The questions and the bar follow the level.

What should I prepare?

Have Flutter and your editor ready, and be able to talk through one app you built. The coding task is short and uses plain Dart and Flutter.

Will this guarantee I clear my interview?

No. A mock interview is practice, not a real hiring interview. It shows you where you stand and what to improve, but no one can guarantee you'll clear an interview or get an offer. See the mock interview terms.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel here at least 24 hours before for a full refund. Closer than that there's no refund, but message us on WhatsApp and we'll try to move you to another time once. If you don't join within 15 minutes, the session counts as missed. See the refund policy.

Do you do Android or iOS interviews?

Not right now: mock interviews are for Flutter only.
