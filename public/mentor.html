FAQ

Questions mentors ask

Do I have to leave my job?

No. Batches run on weekends or weekday evenings, so mentors keep their full-time jobs. Please check that your employer allows part-time teaching.

How much time does it take?

A typical batch is 10 sessions of about 2 hours over 5 weekends, plus a little preparation. The course kit saves most of the prep time.

Do I need teaching experience?

No. Real industry experience matters most. The demo class helps us both see if it's a good fit, and we share tips before your first batch.

Can I show my company name on my profile?

Yes, as text (for example "8 years in IT, ex-product company engineer"), and only with your permission. We don't use company logos.

When and how do I get paid?

After Session 3 of each batch, once the student refund window has closed, by bank transfer or UPI. The exact rates are shared on your call.

Who owns the course material?

The course kit belongs to Widgetry Academy. If you create a new course with us, you earn a royalty every time it runs, even when another mentor teaches it.
