Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

We want you to join with confidence. That's why we offer a free demo class before you pay, and a clear refund policy if the course isn't right for you.

Short crash courses

For short courses such as the Bloc crash course and Flutter, Bloc & Clean Architecture:

  • Cancel before the first session starts: 100% of the amount paid.
  • After the first session has started: no refund.

If we cancel or reschedule

  • If we cancel a batch, you get a full refund, or you can move to the next batch.
  • If a session is rescheduled by us, it will be held at another time. No refund is due for rescheduled sessions.

How to request a refund

Send a cancellation request with your name, registered phone number and payment ID (from your Razorpay receipt) using the details on our Contact page.

Processing time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method. Your bank or UPI app may take a few more days to show the amount.

Booking amount

If you paid the ₹2,500 booking amount, refunds are calculated on the amount actually paid, using the schedule above.

Non-refundable cases

Missing sessions without cancelling, or removal for misconduct under our Terms, is not eligible for a refund.
