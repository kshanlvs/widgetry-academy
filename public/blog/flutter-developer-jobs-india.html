Flutter Developer Jobs in India (2026): Where to Find Them and How to Get Hired

Where Flutter jobs in India are posted, which cities hire most, the skills employers ask for, and a step-by-step plan to land your next Flutter role.

Kishan Kumar Sharma16 October 2026 · 5 min read

Flutter is no longer a "nice extra" on a mobile developer's resume. Google says nearly 30% of new free iOS apps are now built with it, and companies from Google Pay to BMW ship Flutter apps in production. In India, a quick search on LinkedIn shows thousands of open Flutter roles at any given time. The demand is real.

But most job seekers still hunt the hard way: scrolling one big portal, applying to everything, and hearing nothing back. This guide shows where Flutter jobs are actually posted, what employers ask for, and how to get shortlisted.

How many Flutter jobs are there in India?

Job counts change every day, so treat these as snapshots rather than fixed numbers. In October 2026:

  • LinkedIn shows 3,000+ Flutter jobs in India, and 4,000+ roles for mobile application developers in general.
  • Indeed lists around 700 Flutter developer vacancies.
  • Glassdoor lists around 600 Flutter developer jobs.

The numbers overlap, because the same job is often posted on several sites. A fair reading is that there are one to three thousand genuine Flutter openings in India at any moment, plus several thousand more native Android and iOS roles, many of which also welcome Flutter experience.

Where to find Flutter jobs

1. Company careers pages (the most underrated source)

Many product companies post roles first, or only, on their own careers page. Behind those pages are hiring systems such as Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Ashby, Keka and Zoho Recruit. Applying there puts your resume straight into the recruiter's system, with no middleman.

A simple trick: search Google for "Flutter" site:jobs.lever.co India or "Flutter developer" site:jobs.smartrecruiters.com. You will find roles that never reach the big portals.

2. Niche job boards

Small, focused boards have fewer applicants per role and less spam. Widgetry Jobs lists only Flutter, Android and iOS roles, and every listing is reviewed by a working engineer before it goes live. You can filter by job type, work mode, experience, city and skill (Bloc, Riverpod, Firebase and so on).

3. LinkedIn Jobs

Still the biggest source. Two tips:

  • Use the "Past week" filter. Roles older than two weeks are often already in the interview stage.
  • Turn on job alerts for "Flutter developer" in your city and for "Remote".

4. Naukri, Indeed and Instahyre

Good for volume, especially at service companies and agencies. Keep your profile updated weekly: recruiters on these sites sort by "recently active".

5. Startup platforms and internships

Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) and Cutshort list many startup roles. For internships, Internshala is the largest source, and many Flutter internships convert to full-time offers.

Which cities hire the most Flutter developers?

Bengaluru has the largest number of Flutter roles, followed by Hyderabad, Pune, Gurugram/Delhi NCR, Mumbai and Chennai. Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Indore and Kochi have a growing number of roles, mostly at app development agencies and startups.

Remote roles exist, but they usually ask for 2+ years of experience and at least one shipped app. Freshers have a better chance with on-site or hybrid roles.

What skills employers actually ask for

We read through current Flutter job descriptions from Indian companies while building Widgetry Jobs. These requirements come up again and again:

Skill How often it appears Notes
Flutter & Dart (widgets, layouts, navigation) Almost always Expected even for interns
State management (Bloc, Provider, Riverpod or GetX) Almost always Bloc and Provider are the most requested
REST APIs and JSON Almost always Dio or http, error handling, pagination
Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics) Very often Push notifications are a common ask
Clean Architecture or MVVM Often for 2+ years Shows you can work on large codebases
Testing (unit and widget tests) Often for mid/senior Integration tests are a plus
Native Android (Kotlin) or iOS (Swift) Sometimes A strong plus for senior roles and SDKs
A published app (Play Store or App Store) Often The single best proof you can ship

If you are a fresher, the last row matters most. One real, published app beats five certificates.

How to get shortlisted: a practical plan

  1. Build one complete app with authentication, an API, state management and at least one native feature (maps, camera or notifications). Publish it, even as a free app.
  2. Put the code on GitHub with a clear README, screenshots and a short "architecture" section.
  3. Write an ATS-friendly resume. Most companies filter resumes with software before a human reads them. Use a single-column layout, standard section names and real numbers in your achievements. Our free resume builder does this for you.
  4. Apply within the first week a job is posted, ideally on the company's own careers page.
  5. Follow up with the recruiter or engineering manager on LinkedIn with a two-line message and a link to your app.
  6. Prepare for the interview. Expect questions on widget lifecycle, state management, async Dart and performance. Our Flutter interview questions guide covers the common ones.

Avoid job scams

Fake "jobs" that ask for a registration fee, training fee or security deposit are common in India. No genuine employer asks you to pay to get a job or an interview. If a listing asks for money, do not pay. Report it. On Widgetry Jobs, posts that mention fees are blocked automatically, and reported jobs are taken down for review.

Frequently asked questions

Can a fresher get a Flutter job in India?

Yes. Many companies hire Flutter interns and freshers, especially agencies and startups. A published app on the Play Store and clean code on GitHub matter more than your degree. Internships are the most common entry point.

Are Flutter jobs better than React Native jobs?

Both are in demand. Flutter is more popular with new developers and startups, while React Native is strong at companies with large web teams. Read our comparison of Flutter vs React Native jobs for numbers.

Do I need to know Android or iOS for a Flutter job?

Not for most junior roles. For senior roles, basic Kotlin or Swift knowledge helps, because you may need to write platform channels or debug native issues.

Where can I find remote Flutter jobs?

LinkedIn (filter by "Remote"), Wellfound and company careers pages are the best sources. On Widgetry Jobs, choose "Remote" in the work mode filter.
