About Widgetry Jobs

Widgetry Jobs is a job board only for mobile developers in India: Flutter jobs first, plus Android (Kotlin), iOS (Swift) and React Native roles. You'll find full-time jobs, internships for freshers and students, remote Flutter jobs and freelance projects, from entry level to tech lead.

It's run by Widgetry Academy in Kolkata. A working mobile engineer reads every post before it goes live, and fake or paid "jobs" are removed. Job seekers never pay anything, and companies can post a job for free.

Questions

Is Widgetry Jobs free?

Yes. Searching and applying is free for job seekers, always. Posting a job is free for employers too.

Are there Flutter jobs for freshers and internships?

Yes. Choose "Fresher" under experience or "Internship" under job type to see only those.

Can I find remote Flutter jobs?

Choose "Remote" under work mode. Remote jobs here are open to developers anywhere in India.

How do companies find me?

Make a free Talent Network profile and a developer resume. Companies hiring mobile developers can find you there.
