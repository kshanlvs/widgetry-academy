What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, email and photo from Google).
  • Your Talent Network profile and resume, photo, skills and job preferences.
  • Jobs you posted as an employer.
  • Your course registration, unless you paid for it (see below).
  • Your mentor application, if you sent one.
  • Notification tokens for your phones.

Jobs you saved in the app are stored only on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

What we keep, and for how long

If you paid a course fee, we keep the payment record (name, email, amount, date and payment ID) because Indian tax law (GST) requires businesses to keep billing records, currently for about 6 years. It is used only for tax, accounting and refunds, and is deleted when that period ends. Job reports you made stay, without anything that identifies you.

Can't sign in, or need help? Email us from the Contact page with the email address of your account and we'll delete it within 7 days.
