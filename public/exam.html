Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every exam and badge is free.

Do I need an account?

Not to take an exam. If you pass without one, you can create a free account within 7 days and the badge is added to it.

How many times can I try?

3 attempts per level each day. Each attempt draws a fresh set of questions. If you pass again with a higher score, your badge shows the better score and keeps the same link.

Which level should I take?

Junior for up to 2 years of Flutter, mid level for 2–5 years, senior for 5 or more. You can take any level, in any order.

What does the badge prove?

That you passed that Widgetry Flutter exam with the score shown. It's a practice exam, not a job certificate, and it doesn't guarantee a job or an interview result. Anyone with the link can check the name, level, score and date are real.

Can I change the name on my badge?

Yes, under My badges on this page once you're signed in.
