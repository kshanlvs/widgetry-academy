Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The resume, the ATS check, the Classic and Modern templates and the PDF download are free, with no trial. Free resumes have a fixed colour and a small "Made with Widgetry" line at the bottom. Resume Premium is optional: a one-time payment that adds 6 Pro templates, Key highlights, colours, a photo, fit to one page, Word download and AI writing help for 30 days. You can preview Premium templates any time (with a watermark) and download them clean while Premium is on. Nothing renews automatically, and your resume stays yours after it ends.

What happens when Premium ends?

Your resume and everything in it stay yours, and the free templates keep downloading without a watermark. Premium templates go back to a watermarked preview and the AI writer pauses. Buy again any time to switch them back on; if you renew early, the new days are added on top.

How is the ATS score worked out?

We check what screening software and recruiters look for: how many of the job's skills appear on your resume (key skills count double), your title and years of experience, contact details, standard sections, results with numbers, clear bullet points, length and format. Every ATS scores a little differently, so treat it as a guide to improve, not a guarantee.

Does the AI make things up?

No. It only rewrites what's already in your profile and never invents employers, numbers or skills. Where a number would help, it leaves a blank like [X]% for you to fill in. Nothing is saved until you click Apply, and it never sees your phone number or email.

Will ATS software read my resume correctly?

Yes. Every template uses one column, real selectable text, standard section names (Professional Summary, Technical Skills, Professional Experience, Projects, Education) and consistent dates, which is what screening systems expect.

Who can see my profile?

Only you and the Widgetry review team. We share it with an employer only with your approval, and you can pause or delete it any time. See the privacy policy.

I'm a fresher. Will it still look good?

Yes. Add your projects, internships and education; the resume puts your apps and skills first when you don't have work experience yet.
