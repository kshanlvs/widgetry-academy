## How do I pay?

Payments go through Razorpay, so you can pay securely with UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking. Fill in the short registration form first, then pay to confirm your seat.

## Is there a refund?

Yes. If the course isn't right for you, you can get a refund within the first sessions. The exact rules are in our refund policy.

## How long is the course?

It's 10 live online sessions spread over 5 weekends: one every Saturday and Sunday, 2 hours each (20 hours in total). Each session is mostly hands-on building, with time for doubts.

## What if I miss a class?

No problem. Message us and we'll help you catch up before the next class, so you don't fall behind.

## How many students per batch?

We keep batches small, so every student gets individual attention and doubts are solved in class.

## I've never coded. Can I join?

Yes. The course starts from zero: Day 1 is setup and your first app, and Day 2 covers the Dart basics you need. If you can use a computer comfortably, you can follow along.

## Who can join?

Anyone from Class 10 through graduation, in any stream: science, commerce or arts. Recent graduates are welcome too.

## What do I need?

A laptop with Windows, macOS or Linux (8 GB RAM recommended) and an Android phone if you have one. We help you install everything on Day 1, and all the tools are free.

## Do I need a Mac or iPhone?

No. Any Windows, macOS or Linux laptop works. We build and test on Android, and the same Flutter code runs on iOS too.

## Can I do this alongside school or college?

Yes. Classes are only on weekends, so they don't clash with school, college or exams on weekdays.

## What will I build?

One real app, built step by step: designed screens, a product list with live data from the internet, wishlist and cart, a checkout form, and login with cloud data using Firebase. On Day 10 you install it on your phone and put the code on GitHub.

## What's in the syllabus?

Five modules, two sessions each: 1) Getting started and Dart basics, 2) Designing screens, 3) Lists, navigation and live data, 4) Saving data and Firebase login, 5) Polish, build the app and demo day.

## Do I get a certificate?

Yes, a Widgetry Academy certificate with a unique ID you can add to your resume and LinkedIn. To earn it, attend at least 8 of the 10 sessions, try the practice tasks, and present your app on demo day.

## Will this help my career?

You leave with a real app, a GitHub portfolio and guidance on presenting them in your resume and LinkedIn. We don't promise placements, but you'll have proof you can build, which is what recruiters look for. The Professional Flutter Program (coming soon) goes further for job-readiness.

## Who teaches the course?

Kishan Kumar Sharma, a Senior Software Engineer at Relevance Lab with an MCA and 5+ years of building Flutter apps. He has worked on apps with 50M+ downloads, including Tata Neu, and teaches the way real teams build apps.

## Is there a free demo class?

Yes. You can join a free demo class first, with no commitment. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll share the next demo slot.

## Can parents attend?

Yes. Parents can follow the curriculum and are warmly invited to demo day to see the app their child built.

## How do I register?

Tap Reserve my seat, fill in the short form (it takes under a minute), then pay to confirm. You'll get batch details on WhatsApp.

## What is Flutter?

Flutter is Google's free toolkit for building Android, iOS and web apps from one codebase. Apps like Google Pay use it, and it's beginner-friendly: hot reload shows every change on your phone instantly.

## Can I teach at Widgetry?

If you're a working IT professional who enjoys teaching, apply to become a mentor. We handle students, payments and marketing; you teach.

## How do I contact you?

The quickest way is WhatsApp. We usually reply within a few hours. You can also use the contact page.
